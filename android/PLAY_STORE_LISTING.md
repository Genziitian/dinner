# Google Play Store Listing & Submission Guide — DinePOS

## 📌 1. App Details

- **App Name**: DinePOS — Restaurant Billing & POS
- **Short Description (Max 80 chars)**: Fast, intuitive restaurant POS billing, touch ordering, and sales management.
- **Full Description (Max 4000 chars)**:
```
DinePOS is an ultra-fast, modern, and intuitive restaurant billing and Point of Sale (POS) application designed specifically for restaurants, cafes, food trucks, cloud kitchens, and diners.

Built with native performance and clean Material Design 3, DinePOS streamlines table billing, counter orders, portion and weight-based dishes, receipts, and multi-user staff management with server-authoritative security.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

⚡ TOUCH-OPTIMIZED POS BILLING:
- Instant menu catalog with one-tap portion meals (Full, Half, Quarter), piece counts, and custom weight/volume measures (kg, g, liters).
- Live active cart drawer with instant quantity adjusters and customer details.
- Support for Cash and Online/UPI payments with instant receipt billing.

🧾 CRYPTOGRAPHIC DIGITAL & QR RECEIPTS:
- Automatically generate professional itemized receipts with unique cryptographic QR codes.
- Customers can scan the QR code to view and download their verified digital receipt.
- One-tap receipt sharing via WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

📊 REAL-TIME FINANCIAL ANALYTICS & DASHBOARD:
- Today's live collection summaries with Cash vs. Online / UPI breakdown.
- Financial reports with date filtering (Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, This Month, Last Month, Yearly).
- Complete historical order lookup with instant search and status tracking.

🔒 ENTERPRISE-GRADE SECURITY & DATA INTEGRITY:
- 100% server-authoritative calculations with tamper-proof pricing.
- Multi-role staff access control (Superadmin, Manager, Cashier).
- Atomic sequence order numbering resetting safely every calendar day per restaurant timezone.
- Account lockout and brute-force protection.

📱 MODERN NATIVE ARCHITECTURE:
- High-performance 120fps Jetpack Compose UI with adaptive layouts for phones, foldables, and POS tablets.
- Fast local caching and offline graceful error recovery.
- Connect effortlessly to your local network or cloud-hosted DinePOS backend.

Transform your restaurant operations and eliminate checkout bottlenecks with DinePOS today!
```

  - Data is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS/TLS.
