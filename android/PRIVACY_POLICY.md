# Privacy Policy for DinePOS

**Last updated:** August 31, 2026

## Overview
Our restaurant management and billing software gives restaurant owners complete control over their daily operations from one secure platform.

Owners can register their restaurant, manage billing, track transactions, monitor sales, review audit logs, and oversee staff activities in real time. The system supports role-based access for Owners, Managers, and Cashiers, ensuring each team member only has access to the tools and information required for their role.

From billing and transaction management to staff accountability and operational audits, the platform is designed to make restaurant operations simpler, safer, and easier to manage.

---

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how DinePOS ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application and related point-of-sale services.

## 2. Role-Based Access & Data Isolation
The platform enforces role-based access control and tenant isolation:
- **Owners / Admins**: Complete access to configuration, financial reports, user management, audit logs, and analytics.
- **Managers**: Access to inventory, daily orders, reporting, and staff shifts.
- **Cashiers**: Access strictly limited to POS order creation, receipt printing, and shift totals.

## 3. Information We Collect
- **Account & Authentication Information**: When staff members or managers sign in, username and authentication credentials are used strictly to authenticate and authorize access to your restaurant's dedicated database.
- **Order & Transaction Data**: Information related to orders processed through the application, including items purchased, portions, quantities, timestamps, transaction totals, and payment method categories (e.g. Cash, Online/UPI).
- **Customer Information (Optional)**: If entered by staff at the point of sale, customer names or phone numbers are stored strictly for receipt generation and customer order lookup. We do not sell, rent, or monetize customer phone numbers or names.

## 4. How We Use Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Process and record restaurant sales orders.
- Provide accurate financial reporting and sales summaries to authorized managers and store owners.
- Generate digital and printable receipts with cryptographic QR code validation.
- Protect the security of the application and prevent unauthorized access or brute-force attacks.

## 5. Data Protection & Security
- All network communications between the mobile application and the DinePOS server utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- Client-side authentication tokens are securely encrypted using Android Keystore-backed storage (`EncryptedSharedPreferences`).
- Passwords on the backend are hashed using standard cryptographic algorithms (Bcrypt).

## 6. Third-Party Sharing
DinePOS does not sell, trade, or share your data or your customers' data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion
Restaurant owners have full ownership and administrative control of their data. Account and historical transaction data can be exported or purged upon request to the restaurant administrator.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the DinePOS administrator or your restaurant's IT support team.
